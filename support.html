The result doesn't look like my room

HomeGPT keeps the walls, windows and camera angle, and changes materials, lighting and furniture. Photos taken straight on, with the corners of the room visible and decent daylight, give the most faithful results. Very dark or very close-up shots give the model too little to work with.

Nothing happened when I tapped Generate

Generation needs a connection and takes roughly 40 seconds. If it fails, your free design is not used up. Check your connection and try again; if it keeps failing, email us.

The app asks me to subscribe

The first design is free. After that, designing needs a PRO subscription.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, so they are managed on your device. On iPhone: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling there stops future renewals; access continues until the paid period ends. We cannot cancel or refund on your behalf — refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com or from Google Play through your order history.

I paid but PRO isn't active

Open Profile and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase, then email us.

Deleting my data

Designs are stored on your device; deleting them in the app or deleting the app removes them. We keep no account and no copies of your photos. See the privacy policy for detail.
