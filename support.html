The result doesn't look like my room

Casa keeps the walls, windows and camera angle, and changes materials, lighting and furniture. Photos taken straight on, with the corners of the room visible and decent daylight, give the most faithful results. Very dark or very close-up shots give the model too little to work with.

Nothing happened when I tapped Generate

Generation needs a connection and takes roughly 20–30 seconds. If it fails, no credit is spent. Check your connection and try again; if it keeps failing, email us.

I've run out of credits

Free accounts get a set number of generations each day, and the allowance refills automatically. The app shows how long is left until the reset. A PRO subscription removes the limit.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are managed on your device: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops future renewals; access continues until the paid period ends. We cannot cancel or refund on your behalf — refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid but PRO isn't active

Open Profile and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase, then email us.

Deleting my data

Designs are stored on your device; deleting them in the app or deleting the app removes them. We keep no account and no copies of your photos. See the privacy policy for detail.
