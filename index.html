Casa AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

The short version. Casa AI has no accounts and asks for no personal details. The photo you choose is sent to our server, forwarded to our AI provider to be redesigned, and returned to you. We do not keep a copy of your photo. We do not sell data to anyone.

Photos you provide

When you use a tool, the photo you pick is uploaded over an encrypted connection to our server and passed straight to our image-generation provider, fal.ai. Our server holds it only in memory for the duration of the request and never writes it to disk or to a database.

The redesigned images that come back are hosted on fal.ai's content network at unguessable URLs so your device can download them. Casa AI then stores the results locally on your device. Deleting a design in the app removes it from your device.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifier. We use the identifier Apple provides to apps from the same vendor (identifierForVendor) to issue an access token and to apply a daily generation limit that keeps costs and abuse under control. It is held in memory on our server for 24 hours and is not linked to you personally.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, which records an anonymous customer identifier and your subscription state.
  • Configuration. Firebase Remote Config is used to choose which version of a screen to show. It receives a Firebase installation identifier.
  • Advertising attribution. If you arrived from an Apple Search Ads campaign, an Apple AdServices token is passed to RevenueCat so we can tell which campaign the install came from. It contains no personal information.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not sell or rent data to anyone.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.

Camera and photo library

Casa AI asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to add a photo, and only the photos you pick are read. You can withdraw the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Service providers

  • fal.ai — AI image generation. Photos are processed here.
  • Railway — hosting for our server.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Google Firebase — remote configuration.
  • Apple — payments, and Search Ads attribution where applicable.

Children

Casa AI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because we hold no account and no personal profile, there is nothing tied to your identity for us to export or erase. Deleting the app removes the designs stored on your device. If you want the device identifier associated with your usage cleared sooner than its automatic 24-hour expiry, write to us and we will do it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
