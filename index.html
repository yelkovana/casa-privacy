HomeGPT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version. HomeGPT (AI Home Design) has no accounts and asks for no personal details. The photo you choose is sent to our server, forwarded to our AI providers to be read and redesigned, and the result is returned to you. We do not keep a copy of your photo. We do not sell data to anyone.

Photos you provide

When you use a tool, the photo you pick is uploaded over an encrypted connection to our server. Our server holds it only in memory for the duration of the request and never writes it to disk or to a database. It passes the photo to two providers:

  • kie.ai generates the redesigned image. The photo is placed in kie.ai's temporary file store, which deletes it after 24 hours, and the finished image is hosted at an unguessable temporary address so your device can download it.
  • Google (Gemini API) reads the room in your photo, and the finished design, to produce the short notes the app shows you (room type, what was noticed, what is in the design).

HomeGPT then stores the results on your device. For interior designs it also keeps the original photo on your device so you can compare before and after. Deleting a design in the app, or deleting the app, removes them.

Information collected automatically

  • Device identifier. On iPhone we use the identifier Apple provides to apps from the same vendor (identifierForVendor); on Android the app creates a random identifier the first time it runs. It is used to issue an access token and to apply a daily generation limit that keeps costs and abuse under control. It is held in memory on our server for 24 hours and is not linked to you personally.
  • Subscription status. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google Play and managed through RevenueCat, which records an anonymous customer identifier and your subscription state.
  • Configuration, usage and crashes. Google Firebase is used to choose which version of a screen to show (Remote Config), to count how the app is used (Analytics) and to receive crash reports (Crashlytics). These receive a Firebase installation identifier, basic device information and, for a crash, the technical details of the failure.
  • Advertising attribution. So we can tell which campaign an install came from: on iPhone, an Apple AdServices token from an Apple Search Ads campaign is passed to RevenueCat; on Android, the Google Play install referrer and the device's advertising ID are passed to RevenueCat. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Reports. If you report a design in the Android app, the reason you chose and your device identifier are recorded so we can review it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not require an account.
  • We do not sell or rent data to anyone.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.

Camera and photos

On iPhone, HomeGPT asks for camera and photo library access only when you choose to add a photo, and only the photos you pick are read; you can withdraw the permission at any time in iOS Settings. On Android, photos are chosen through the system photo picker and the system camera, so the app can only see the single photo you select and needs no permission to your library. Saving a design writes it to your own gallery.

Service providers

  • kie.ai — AI image generation. Photos are processed here.
  • Google (Gemini API) — reading the room and the finished design.
  • Railway — hosting for our server.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management.
  • Google Firebase — remote configuration, analytics and crash reports.
  • Apple and Google Play — payments, and campaign attribution where applicable.

Children

HomeGPT is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Because we hold no account and no personal profile, there is nothing tied to your identity for us to export or erase. Deleting the app removes the designs stored on your device. If you want the device identifier associated with your usage cleared sooner than its automatic 24-hour expiry, write to us and we will do it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
